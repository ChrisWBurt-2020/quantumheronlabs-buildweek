01 THE PROBLEM

The internet is very good at capturing attention. It is not designed to build understanding.

Important ideas arrive mixed with noise. Useful reading gets stranded across tabs and saves. Even the things you understand today quietly disappear by next week.

Heron closes that gap.

01

Noise

Feeds optimize for reaction, not relevance.

02

Fragmentation

Reading and learning live in separate tools.

03

Forgetting

Saving information is not the same as retaining it.

    One recommended next step, clearer paths, mobile-first guided sessions, and a production launch at its canonical domain.

  2. 02

    Unified identity

    HeronFeed and Learn now share one verified Heron account with recovered cross-device confirmation.

  4. 04

  5. 05
    Made the vision legible

    This submission site explains the complete loop and marks the future TruthCast layer without pretending it is finished.

CODEX

GPT-5.6

The editorial decision

The user still chooses the sources, decides what matters, and controls what becomes part of their learning path. Heron assists judgment; it does not replace it.
