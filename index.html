01 THE PROBLEM

The internet is very good at capturing attention. It is not designed to build understanding.

Important ideas arrive mixed with noise. Useful reading gets stranded across tabs and saves. Even the things you understand today quietly disappear by next week.

Heron closes that gap.

01

Noise

Feeds optimize for reaction, not relevance.

02

Fragmentation

Reading and learning live in separate tools.

03

Forgetting

Saving information is not the same as retaining it.
