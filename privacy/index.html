PRIVACY & DATA HANDLING

Private by default.

The short version: this site doesn't track you, and client work stays client work. Your documents, your workflows, your data — never used to train models, never sold, never shared.

Last updated September 30, 2026

This website

  • No analytics, no tracking cookies, no accounts. There is nothing on this site measuring your visit. The only thing that leaves your browser is an email you choose to send.
  • Contact is email. Writing to chris@quantumheronlabs.com puts your message in my inbox, where I read it and reply. That's the whole pipeline.
  • The on-page demo widget (the Heron companion) runs in anonymous mode against my own infrastructure. It doesn't identify you.
  • Hosting. This site is served through Cloudflare from infrastructure I operate. Standard server logs (IP address, requested page, timestamp) exist for security and reliability, and aren't used for profiling.

Pilot engagements

  • Your data stays yours. Everything built during a pilot — including the evidence report — belongs to you, per the engagement terms.
  • Your infrastructure or mine, your choice. The pilot can run entirely on your systems. If it runs on mine, access is scoped to the pilot and revoked at handoff.
  • No training on your data. Documents, workflows, and operational data you provide are used only to build your pilot. They are never used to train models — mine or anyone else's.
  • Minimal access. I ask for the smallest set of documents and system access the workflow needs, and nothing more.
  • End of pilot. At handoff, anything of yours on my infrastructure is returned or deleted — your choice, confirmed in writing.

The Heron platform apps

Watch, Feed, Graph, Learn, and Client are separate applications with their own sign-in. Each will show its own data-use notice where you create an account. Until then, one rule covers all five: your knowledge base is yours, and it isn't used to train shared models.

What I will never do

  • Sell or share your data with third parties.
  • Train models on client documents or operational data.
  • Keep access to your systems after an engagement ends.

Questions

Anything about data handling — for the site, a pilot, or the platform: chris@quantumheronlabs.com. I answer these myself.
